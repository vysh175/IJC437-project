# IJC437 Project – Rail Passenger Demand Volatility by Operator

## Brief introduction (3–4 lines)
This project analyses passenger journey data across rail operators using official time-series datasets.  
The aim is to understand how demand changes over time and quantify volatility across operators.  
The workflow includes data cleaning, visual exploration, volatility measurement, and clustering in R.

## Research questions
1. To what extent does passenger rail usage remain stable over time across UK operators? 
2. Which operators experience the greatest variation in passenger demand over time?
3. Can train operators be grouped into stable, moderately volatile, and highly volatile demand clusters? 

## Key findings
- Passenger journeys differ significantly across operators, with a small group consistently showing much higher volumes.
- Volatility (measured using the standard deviation of passenger journeys) varies widely between operators.
- Clustering identifies groups of operators with low, medium, and high volatility, highlighting structural differences in demand patterns.
